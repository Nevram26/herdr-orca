# Changes in this copy

This mod is `t3-actions` from [JinJieBeWater/herdr-t3](https://github.com/JinJieBeWater/herdr-t3)
(Apache-2.0), part of the Herdr T3 layout, modified for use on its own and in the Orca layout.

- `main.js`, `tip.js` and `check.cjs` are modified versions of the originals, each marked with a
  credit line at the top.
- The action icons sit in the mod's root instead of `icons/`; the original's `focus.js` and
  `list.js` and its other icons are not included.
